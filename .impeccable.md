## Design Context

### Users
UAE professionals aged 22–40 considering credit cards. They want to understand eligibility, fees and rewards before choosing a card. The current launch page should communicate the product clearly and convert interest into a waitlist signup.

### Brand Personality
Candid, discerning, approachable. The client explicitly wants premium quality and excitement; the creator rejects generic AI aesthetics. Clarity and confidence should come from composition and meaningful content.

### Aesthetic Direction
An independent consumer guide with the precision of a printed financial journal. Use original Billd colours (#0A1628, #2E5BFF, #6B8AFF, #A8BDFF, #EDEDEA, #F7F9FF) and preserve the existing circular mark and lowercase identity. Paper surfaces, cobalt ink, editorial typography and freestanding card compositions. The current direction is “unfold the card”: moderately sized offset type, a layered hero object, a separate explanatory card anatomy, and a restrained brand colophon. Avoid large primary-colour section backgrounds, glowing orbs, simulated dashboards, glass surfaces, decorative charts, repetitive slogans, repeated card grids and gratuitous motion.

### Design Principles
1. Explain the product before adding decoration; every visual should connect to credit-card decisions.
2. Build contrast through typography, scale and composition. Keep accents deliberate.
3. Offer signup immediately and once again at the close. Preserve all accessible Formspree states.
4. Use varied section spacing. Pin only the explanatory graphic beside real content on spacious desktops; never reserve an empty viewport for animation or hijack native scrolling.
5. Adapt the composition to mobile and reduced motion. Use original illustrations rather than bank logos or invented product statistics.

### Type Direction
Brand voice: candid, discerning, approachable. Reflex choices considered and rejected: Instrument Serif, Newsreader, Space Grotesk. Catalog access was blocked by the approval service. From the locally available open-font catalog, choose Gloock for the structure and ink-like contrast of a printed guide, paired with existing Geist for readable functional copy. Self-host the font and include its OFL licence.

Use a comfortable, consistent hierarchy: fluid display type capped at 4rem, section headings at 2.75rem, subheadings at 2rem, and fixed 1rem body copy. Scale with rem-plus-viewport formulas without breakpoint jumps. Keep spacing proportional; oversized typography must not dominate the layout.
